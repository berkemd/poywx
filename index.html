POYWX: Signal Cascade

A gem puzzle. Draw a thread of light across neighbouring stones of the same colour, then let go: the thread burns, and its last stone takes the rest of its colour with it.

No servers

No account, no email address. The game runs on your device and stays there.

No ads

No interstitial videos, no tracking, no advertising identifier.

Free to play

Five chapters of levels, the daily tray and three arcade modes are free. Pro opens Endless and the sixth chapter.

One rule

The rule fits in a sentence, and what will happen is written under the tray before you let go.
